# Feature Specification: Floor 50 Hiding Spot Placement

**Status**: Draft  
**GDD Sources**: Ch. 3, 4.3

## User Stories & Acceptance

### US1 — Use rare cover wisely (P1)
Floor 50 has scarce, high-value hiding cover with safe exits that does not trivialize the aggressive route.

**Independent test**: chase the player through each spot and verify entry/exit, immunity, and objective access.

## Functional Requirements

- FR-001: Spot count and locations MUST match the Floor 50 design decision and be validated in scene review.
- FR-002: Every spot MUST have safe anchors and connect to the route without a trap.
- FR-003: Placement MUST preserve pressure and remain resettable.

## Success Criteria

- SC-001: Anchor/collision/no-trap audit passes.
- SC-002: Playtesters use cover tactically without bypassing the three-door objective.

## Scope

Scene placement only.
