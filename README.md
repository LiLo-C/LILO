# LILO: Lights In Lights Out

LILO is a mobile survival horror game set in a dark office. Explore each floor, find the access key, keep your flashlight powered, and reach the exit while avoiding the creature hunting nearby.

## How to play

- Move through the office and search desks and rooms for the floor's access key and spare batteries.
- Use the flashlight to see, but keep an eye on its charge. Replace its battery when it runs low.
- Be mindful of the noise you make. The creature can hear you and may investigate or give chase.
- Hide when danger is close, then make your way to the exit. Locked exits require the access key.
- You have a limited number of lives, so each escape matters.

The game includes three office floors and story scenes with branching outcomes.

## Controls

Use the on-screen joystick to move. Use the context action button when it appears to interact with nearby objects or enter and leave hiding spots. Sprint, when available, by pushing the joystick farther; moving quickly can attract attention.
